06 / QUICK REFERENCE

Technical FAQ

Short answers to common questions. For a definitive answer, consult your vehicle manufacturer.

Have a model-specific question?
Contact an authorized service center.

Check for an E20 label on the fuel flap, consult the owner's manual for your exact model and year, or ask an authorized service center. A vehicle's manufacture date alone does not confirm approval.

For vehicles approved for E20, normal mixing with lower-ethanol petrol is generally not a concern. For older or non-approved vehicles, follow the manufacturer's fuel guidance rather than relying on a universal rule.

Ethanol has high knock resistance, which can raise a blend's octane rating. However, ethanol contains less energy per litre than petrol, so a litre of E20 generally provides less energy even as its knock resistance improves.

Warranty terms are manufacturer- and model-specific. Using fuel outside the vehicle's stated specification may affect coverage for related repairs; confirm approved blends and warranty conditions in your manual or with the manufacturer.
